Daqiq (دقیق) — Support

Daqiq brings short lessons drawn from the Quran, verse by verse, in Urdu and English — with the full Quran, Quranic and hadith duas, a hadith library, prayer times with adhan, Qibla and a Hijri calendar.

Contact

For questions, suggestions or to report a mistake in any lesson, translation or date, email akram@inparlor.com. We read every message.

Common questions

Prayer times look a few minutes off. Open Prayer → settings and choose the calculation method and Asr school used in your area; you can also adjust each prayer by minutes.

The Hijri date is a day different from my local announcement. Open the Hijri calendar and use “Date adjustment” (−2 to +2 days).

Adhan alerts don't sound. Allow notifications for Daqiq in your phone's Settings, and keep Daqiq's alert mode set to Adhan for that prayer.

Adding the widgets (iPhone). Open Daqiq once, then long-press the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Daqiq.
